Limits and team

Honest limits

What to keep in mind when reading the results.

  • Radar isn’t a photo. Bright and dark areas show how the ground scatters radio waves, not colour.
  • Numbers are estimates. Change figures come from comparing two passes and can be off at the edges.
  • Not every change is a flood or a loss. Wind, farming and wet soil can also alter the signal.
  • Passes are days apart. Anything that happened between them is not shown.
